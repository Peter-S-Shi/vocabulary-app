# User-Owned Content Policy

Vocabulary App is software for organizing and practicing language-learning content supplied by its users.

## What This App Provides

The app provides local tools for:

- vocabulary entry and template management
- collections and card organization
- review scheduling
- self-graded, multiple-choice, and matching quizzes
- Mistake Book, Starred, and Proficient Pool workflows
- statistics and Review Calendar views
- CSV/XLSX import and export
- local backup and restore preview
- a daily learning workflow through Today

## What This App Does Not Provide

The project does not ship with:

- dictionary databases
- commercial or copyrighted word lists
- example-sentence databases
- pronunciation audio libraries
- voice or TTS models
- AI-generated entries, definitions, examples, or default datasets
- automatic linguistic correction

The app is not a dictionary authority, pronunciation service, AI language tutor, or cloud vocabulary provider.

## User Responsibility

Users create and maintain their own learning content. Users are responsible for:

- reviewing the accuracy of terms, meanings, translations, examples, and notes
- deciding when an entry is ready for review or proficiency workflows
- ensuring they have permission to use content they enter or import
- protecting local databases, exports, and backup files

The app can validate file structure and support learning workflows, but it does not verify linguistic accuracy, copyright status, or source permissions.

## Import / Export Responsibility

CSV and XLSX imports should contain content that the user created, collected for permitted personal study, or otherwise has the right to use.

Exports and backups may reproduce personal notes and imported content. Review files before sharing them and do not publish personal databases in public repositories or support requests.

## No Bundled Dictionary, Audio, or AI Content

Avoiding bundled language-content databases is a deliberate product choice. It:

- preserves local data ownership
- avoids hidden licensing and copyright assumptions
- keeps the app usable across languages and custom templates
- prevents external services from becoming required for core learning workflows

## Future Optional Extensions

Future extensions may be considered only when they preserve:

- local-first behavior
- explicit user control
- clear licensing boundaries
- no hidden bundled copyrighted content
- backward-compatible software updates
- a functional core without mandatory AI or cloud services
